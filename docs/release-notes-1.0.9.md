# Ghost City RLT 1.0.9

## Updated tools

- Updated **Dynamic Recruiting Pipelines** to v1.2.0 while retaining Ghost City's hardened save wrapper. The update repairs stale `Unrecognized` pipeline references, prevents valid rows after damaged slots from being reused, and rebuilds pipeline ownership after an integrity repair.
- Updated **Dynamic Conference Realignment** to v0.2.0 settings. Toolbox now includes Hawai‘i travel treatment plus target sizes for Power 4, Pac-12, and Group of 5 conferences.

## Notes

- Dynamic Conference Realignment still produces recommendations only. It does not change conference membership in a dynasty save; apply any accepted moves through CFB 27's Custom Conferences menu during the offseason.
- The preseason workflow remains: **Fang → Dynamic Recruiting Pipelines → Transfer Wave → CFB Rebalance → Import**.
- Replace your previous portable EXE with the new one. Existing Ghost City history, settings, and backups remain intact.
